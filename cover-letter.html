Cover Letter

AKA Why You Should Hire Me.


Dear Director of People Who Can Make Things Happen,

I write code the way some people solve puzzles: with curiosity, a touch of stubbornness, and an oddly satisfying sense of joy when things finally click. As a software engineering student at Western Governors University, I'm looking for opportunities to turn my growing skills into real-world magic.

I’ve dabbled in SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python. I've built various websites, I've crafted my own personal portfolio from scratch, and I single-handedly designed and created a custom CRM database for my own massage therapy business. I’m not afraid to ask the hard questions (like “Why isn't this working?” and “Did I really forget a semicolon again?”). Whether it’s building clean user interfaces or wrangling logic bugs at 2 a.m., I’m all in.

If your team could use a fast learner who writes clean code, loves a challenge, and brings snacks to meetings, I’d love to connect.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
Stephanie Valdez
